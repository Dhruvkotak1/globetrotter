Top Money & Forex Tips for Traveling Abroad

Master your travel finances before stepping onto international flights.

Zero Forex Markup Cards

Use zero-forex debit or credit cards (like Niyo, Fi, or Scapia) instead of regular cards which charge up to 3.5% + GST markup per swipe.

Always Pay in Local Currency

When POS card machines abroad ask whether to charge in INR or Local Currency, always choose Local Currency to avoid dynamic currency conversion (DCC) fee penalties.

Emergency Cash & LRS Limits

Indian travelers are permitted to carry up to $3,000 (or equivalent in foreign currency notes) in cash under RBI Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS).
